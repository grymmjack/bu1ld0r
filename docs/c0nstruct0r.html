01 What it is

c0nstruct0r was built to rebuild NEON-CASTER's props. Most of them are paintings pushed out into slabs, so their sides and tops show one row of pixels smeared across them. c0nstruct0r builds them again from boxes, wedges and cylinders, with insets and extrusions for screens and panels, and maps the painting onto each face at the right size. It works on any bu1ld0r model, not only game props.

Three parts work together:

Claude Codewith the /c0nstruct0r skill: the method to follow
tools
MCP servertools/c0nstruct0r/mcp_server.py, plain Python, started by .mcp.json
port
bu1ld0rstarted with --port: listens on 127.0.0.1:47800, a line in, a line out

Every command is an ordinary bu1ld0r operation (add a part, pick faces, inset, extrude, map a picture) and each one is a single undo step. After Claude finishes, Ctrl+Z steps back through everything it did.

How it works

02 What you need

  • bu1ld0r: a download or your own build. Downloads include everything c0nstruct0r needs beside the program: tools/c0nstruct0r/, the skill in .claude/skills/c0nstruct0r/, and .mcp.json. Keep them in bu1ld0r's folder.
  • Claude Code, installed and signed in (claude in a terminal). c0nstruct0r uses your own account.
  • Python 3. It needs nothing beyond the standard library, so there's nothing to pip install.
  • Optional: NEON-CASTER, for rebuilding its props. The painting and check tools read the game's paintings and models from ~/git/neon-caster/neon-caster, or from wherever NEON_CASTER_DIR points. Without it, everything else still works, and Claude uses the pictures already on your model's faces.

Platforms. The port and the MCP server work on Linux, macOS and Windows. ASK CLAUDE inside FIX THIS works on Linux and macOS for now, because it starts Claude through a small shell script.

03 FIX THIS (F2)

Press F2, or FIX in the top bar, to open a panel on the right. It checks the model as EXPORT would write it, including hidden parts, and tidied if EXPORT: TIDIED is on. It gives a SCORE: 0 means nothing was found, and the game's own props score between 40 and 90. The panel checks again a moment after each change you make.

Each finding has a row, and each row has the same buttons:

  • SHOW selects the parts that have this finding.
  • LEAVE means you want it as it is. The finding drops out of the score, and that choice is saved with the model (fixleave smear in the .bld).
  • The fix, where bu1ld0r can make one without Claude. It works on the selected parts, or on the whole model if nothing is selected, and is one undo step.
FindingWhat it meansFix
SMEARA picture pulled across a face into streaks, stretched 3:1 or more over pixels that differMAKE PLAIN bu1ld0r gives each smeared face its pixels' average colour.
PICTURES Claude maps it again from the painting.
PIXEL SIZEFaces whose pixels are 1.5× bigger or smaller than the model's usual sizePICTURES Claude: choosing a new piece of the picture is a judgement call
ANGLESFaces between 0.75° and 8° off square or 45°. A round thing's facets don't count.SQUARE UP bu1ld0r snaps the turns, then makes each face lie exactly square
SYMMETRYThe outline seen from the front and from above, compared with its mirror image. The panel draws it, lit where the two sides don't match.KEEP L / KEEP R bu1ld0r keeps one half, as seen from the front, and mirrors it over the other. A part that crosses the middle is cut there and rebuilt from its kept half.
TRIANGLESThe count against the budget of 1000TIDY bu1ld0r turns on EXPORT: TIDIED at that budget

ASK CLAUDE

Below the findings, the panel has a box headed What should I fix? and three buttons. This part appears only when bu1ld0r can find claude and has tools/c0nstruct0r/ and .mcp.json beside it.

ButtonClaude does
PICTURESMaps the faces flagged by SMEAR, PIXEL SIZE and REPEATS again, from the painting if there is one, else from the pictures already on the model. It uses one picture for the whole model, and a plain colour where nothing fits.
DETAILGives flat slabs the detail the painting shows (insets, extrusions, bevels), then maps the new faces
REBUILDTakes the selected parts, or the whole model, through the method again, leaving the rest alone
  1. Select parts first if you only want those worked on. Type anything Claude should know in the box, for example "keep the screen as it is". Your words come first in what Claude reads.
  2. Press a button. bu1ld0r saves a backup to c0nstruct0r/backup/, opens its port, and starts claude -p in the background. Claude can use only the c0nstruct0r tools: no shell and no file edits, only bu1ld0r through its port.
  3. Watch the strip at the bottom of the view. It shows what Claude is doing and why. Your keyboard and mouse are locked out, except for Esc.
  4. When Claude finishes, the panel shows the score before and after, and what Claude says it changed.

One example from testing: PICTURES on a rebuilt console took its score from 25 to 3 in about four minutes. Claude re-mapped the cabinet sides from a patch of the painting at the front's pixel size, and gave the beacon and antenna plain colours.

04 A Claude Code session

For a whole rebuild, or anything you'd rather discuss first:

  1. Start bu1ld0r with its port open: ./bu1ld0r.run --port, or put port=1 in bu1ld0r.cfg to open it every time. It writes c0nstruct0r.port holding the port number and a token.
  2. In a terminal, in bu1ld0r's folder, run claude. The first time, Claude Code asks whether to allow the project's c0nstruct0r MCP server. Say yes.
  3. Ask for what you want:
# rebuild a NEON-CASTER prop from its painting
/c0nstruct0r crate

# or in your own words, on the model that's open
take the model in bu1ld0r and give the lid a bevel and a plank picture

For a prop, the skill follows these steps:

  1. Read the painting, measured on a grid. The subject's height in pixels against the game's height gives the scale.
  2. Plan one part per visible block, sized from the painting and centred on x = 0, with a say line giving the reason for each.
  3. No gaps. Parts rest on each other. A sloped slab gets a wedge under it.
  4. Detail with cheap moves: inset, extrude, mirror.
  5. Pictures from one picture, the painting: the fronts as seen, the sides and tops from matching strips of it, plain colours where the painting shows nothing.
  6. Check the game's eight views against the painting, and the report card.
  7. Keep: it saves to models/c0-NAME.bld and exports to export/. It writes into the game's folder only when you ask.

Rehearse hidden. The skill tries a new step file on a hidden copy of bu1ld0r first (a second display), and moves to your screen only when you ask to watch. A step file that fails partway is fixed there, not in front of you.

05 While it works

While c0nstruct0r has taken the model, a strip over the foot of the view says what it's doing. bu1ld0r ignores your keys, clicks, wheel and even pointer moves, so nothing you do can get in its way.

Keep the lid's planks, or make the lid plain?

keepplain
c0nstruct0r: the lid a touch bigger, so its edge showsEsc takes it back

An ask box over the strip. Clicking an answer is the one click let through the lock.

You pressWhat happens
EscTakes the model back at once, as it is. Claude's next command gets err stopped. When it was started from ASK CLAUDE, its process is ended too.
F9After an Esc, lets it carry on
An answer buttonAnswers its ask. It waits as long as you take.
Ctrl+ZAfterwards: undoes its changes one step at a time

It pauses briefly after each change (pace, 250 ms by default) so you can follow along. Closing the connection hands the model back too.

06 The report card

tools/c0nstruct0r/report.py grades OBJ files, the game's or bu1ld0r's exports, with the same checks FIX THIS runs inside bu1ld0r. The two give the same scores.

# every prop in the game, worst first
python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/report.py

# your export against the game's own
python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/report.py --models export crate

The score is smear + asym + (size + angle) / 2 + over. Each term is a percentage, and over is how far the triangles go past the budget, capped at 200. Lower is better. The props c0nstruct0r has rebuilt so far score between 0 and 32, against the game's 42 to 64 for the same props.

07 Driving it yourself

rc.py connects to the port, sends the token, and prints each reply:

python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/rc.py take                # one command
python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/rc.py -i                  # type commands, one per line
python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/rc.py -f tools/c0nstruct0r/props/crate.txt
python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/rc.py --dir /path/to/bu1ld0r -f steps.txt

A step file is a list of port commands, one per line, stopping at the first err. Here is the start of the crate's:

take
pace 400
say crate: a wooden crate, strapped, its lid a band of its own
new force
add box 0.0039 0.0118 0.0 0.4475 0.208 0.38 col=8A5A30 name=body
ok 1
add box 0.0039 0.2198 0.0 0.4671 0.2022 0.4 col=A8743C name=lid
ok 2
edit 1
pick facing 0 0 -1 45
picture c0nstruct0r/crate view 0 14 122 234 224 own
ok
...the back, sides and lid the same way...
done
fix
ok score 0 smear 0 size 0 angle 0 asym 0 tris 24/1000 pics 1 repeats 0 ...
give

The props' step files are written by tools/c0nstruct0r/props/recipes.py, one recipe per prop, which describes each part as a box in the painting's pixels. Run python3 tools/c0nstruct0r/props/recipes.py NAME to write props/NAME.txt.

08 Port commands

One line in, one line out: ok ... or err with the reason. help lists them all. A command that changes the model needs take first.

CommandDoes
hello TOKENAlways first (rc.py and the MCP server send it for you)
take / giveTake the model, locking out everything but Esc / hand it back
say TEXTSets the strip's words: what it's doing and why
pace MSSets the pause after each change (250 by default, 0 to 5000)
ask Q | A | BAsks you, with a button per answer (yes / no by default, up to four). Replies ok A once you click, or err stopped on Esc.
status, parts, faces [P]JSON: the model and camera; each part; each face of the part being edited
shot NAME [win | clean]Saves a picture to c0nstruct0r/shots/NAME.png: the view, the whole window, or the model alone
new, open NAME [force]Starts or opens a model (neon:PROP opens a game prop). force works even when the model isn't saved.
save [NAME], exportSaves to models/; exports to export/ only
undo, redoAs Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
add SHAPE [X Y Z [SX SY SZ [RX RY RZ]]] [key=value...]Adds a part and replies with its number. Keys: x y z sx sy sz rx ry rz col amt seg name hole keep hide group
set P key=value...Changes a part (every key or none)
del P..., sel none | all | P...Deletes; selects
look all | P..., view YAW [PITCH [DIST]]Turns the camera to show them / to that angle (yaw 0 is the front)
mesh P, mirror P [x|y|z] [at=V]Makes a shape a mesh part; makes a mirrored copy
edit P / doneStarts editing part P's faces / stops
pick [add] none | all | F... | facing X Y Z [DEG]Picks faces: by number, or those facing a way (within 10° unless DEG says otherwise)
extrude D, inset K, push DActs on the picked faces: out by D with sides (negative goes in); each inset K of the way to its middle; pushed along its way
move DX DY DZ, scale K [KY KZ]Moves or scales the picked faces (outside editing: the selected parts)
colour RRGGBB, flipSets a colour; turns the faces over
picture REF fit | tile N | view YAW X0 Y0 X1 Y1 [own] | top X0 Y0 X1 Y1 [own] | nonePuts pictures/REF.png on the picked faces: stretched to fit; repeated at N pixels a cell; as seen from YAW with the model's box filling that rectangle (top: from above); own uses the part's box instead of the model's
fix [check | open | close | plain | square | keep left | keep right | tidy | show W | leave W]FIX THIS: the report card's numbers, its panel, a fix, or SHOW / LEAVE a finding (W: smear size angle asym tris pics repeats)

Shapes: box cylinder sphere cone wedge tube die star gear donut diamond spike pill arrow. A shape is 1 × 1 × 1 standing on its foot, sized by sx sy sz, turned Z, then X, then Y.

To point the server at another copy of bu1ld0r, start it with --dir FOLDER or set C0NSTRUCT0R_DIR.

10 Files it writes

Everything goes in bu1ld0r's own folder:

c0nstruct0r.port
The port number and this run's token. Only programs on this computer can connect.
c0nstruct0r/shots/
Pictures taken by shot and look
c0nstruct0r/backup/
The model as it was before each ASK CLAUDE
c0nstruct0r/fix-*.txt
ASK CLAUDE's prompt, Claude's output, its process id, and how it ended
models/c0-NAME.bld
A rebuilt prop, which you can keep editing
pictures/c0nstruct0r/
The paintings it maps from
export/
Exports. It never writes into the game's folder unless you ask.

Safety. The port listens on 127.0.0.1 only and turns away connections from anywhere else. Every connection must send the token first. ASK CLAUDE runs Claude with the c0nstruct0r tools alone, so it can change your model only through bu1ld0r, one undo step at a time.

11 Troubleshooting

You seeTry
No ASK CLAUDE rowbu1ld0r looks for claude in ~/.local/bin, ~/.claude/local, /usr/local/bin, /opt/homebrew/bin and /usr/bin, and needs tools/c0nstruct0r/mcp_server.py and .mcp.json beside it. On Windows it isn't available yet.
The tools say bu1ld0r isn't runningStart it with --port and check that c0nstruct0r.port appeared in its folder
"port didn't open"Ports 47800 to 47809 are all in use, probably by other copies of bu1ld0r. Close one, or set port= to a free number.
err take firstSend take before commands that change the model
err stoppedYou pressed Esc. F9 lets it carry on.
err not savednew and open refuse to drop unsaved work. Save first, or add force.
painting or check can't find the gameSet NEON_CASTER_DIR to the folder that holds the game's hd/ and models/

For more detail, C0NSTRUCT0R.md has the plan, the decisions behind it, and every prop rebuilt so far. The skill is .claude/skills/c0nstruct0r/SKILL.md.
